LEADKAUN — PRIORITY QUEUE PLAYBOOK
===================================
How to give every rep one ranked list of who to call next — manual first,
automated second. A queue exists to protect the response window on your best
leads, so the hot lead is the one your rep calls at minute five, not position 38.

--------------------------------------------------
THE 4-SIGNAL RANK FORMULA
Rank each lead by combining:
  1. GRADE — the A-F score (Fit x Intent x Quality). Grade A floats to the top.
  2. URGENCY — how close the lead is to going cold / past its window.
  3. TREND — is intent rising (recent reply, call) or decaying (silent)?
  4. RECENCY — freshness, as a tie-breaker only — NOT the primary sort.
Common mistake: sorting by recency alone. That buries a warm Grade B that
replied 2 days ago behind a cold new form-fill.

GRADE WEIGHTS THAT WORK FOR INDIAN B2B SMBs
  Fit:     industry (30) + geography (20) + business type (20) + role (15) + budget (15)
  Intent:  source baseline + call (+15) + WhatsApp reply (+10) + meeting (+25),
           minus ~3/day decay after the engagement threshold
  Quality: valid phone (30) + email (15) + company (15) + inquiry clarity (20) + source (10)

FATIGUE RULE
A lead contacted 6+ times with no positive signal drops to the bottom
(rank score -1) so reps stop burning time on it. Un-fatigue manually if
circumstances change.

MANUAL-TO-AUTOMATED TRANSITION CRITERIA
Move from a spreadsheet queue to an automated one when you hit roughly:
  - 200+ live leads, OR
  - 3+ reps sharing/handing off leads, OR
  - leads arriving from 3+ channels.
Below that, a disciplined spreadsheet works. Above it, manual re-ranking breaks.
