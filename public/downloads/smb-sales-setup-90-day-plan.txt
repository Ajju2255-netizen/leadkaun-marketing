LEADKAUN — 90-DAY SALES SETUP PLAN (INDIAN B2B SMB)
====================================================
A pragmatic plan to go from "leads in a spreadsheet" to a working sales system.
One focus per phase. Don't skip ahead — each phase makes the next one stick.

--------------------------------------------------
DAYS 1-30 — CAPTURE & CLARITY
  [ ] Get every lead into ONE place (stop the five-inboxes problem).
  [ ] Normalise Indian phone numbers and dedupe on import.
  [ ] Write your ICP (see the ICP Worksheet) — who you sell to best.
  [ ] Set a simple A-F grade on each lead (Fit x Intent x Quality).
  [ ] Agree a response-time target for Grade A leads.
  Outcome: no lead is lost, and everyone knows which leads matter.

DAYS 31-60 — PRIORITISE & FOLLOW UP
  [ ] Build a Priority Queue: reps work top-down, not newest-first.
  [ ] Log calls / WhatsApp in a low-friction way (aim for 3 taps).
  [ ] Start a Morning Brief — ₹ at risk + top Grade A + overdue follow-ups.
  [ ] Track follow-up completion per rep.
  Outcome: reps spend hours on the right leads; fewer go cold.

DAYS 61-90 — MEASURE & COACH
  [ ] Run a weekly review off real numbers (see the Weekly Review Template).
  [ ] Track the 4 behaviours that predict revenue: response time, follow-up
      completion, lead selection, ₹ recovered.
  [ ] Coach on specific signals, not effort.
  [ ] Decide what to automate (queue, scoring, alerts) vs keep manual.
  Outcome: a repeatable system you can grow headcount into.

--------------------------------------------------
HIRING SIGNAL: add your second rep when the data shows Grade A leads slipping
uncontacted week after week — a measured bottleneck, not a hunch.
